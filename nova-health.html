Case study 02 · Simulated

Nova Health
Turning account risk into renewal evidence.

An at-risk healthcare account with eight clinics, declining adoption, falling NPS and a renewal six months away. My approach: stabilise, re-engage, demonstrate value.

Health auditDiagnosis90-day planEBRDay 90
01 · Health audit

Amber means: act now.

The account was not lost, but multiple signals were moving in the wrong direction at once.

Nova Health is an eight-clinic network using a patient scheduling and communication platform. The account is nine months into its contract with renewal in six months.

02 · Risk diagnosis

Three risks. Three owners.

I translated the account signals into concrete risks, owners and actions instead of treating “at risk” as a diagnosis.

Risk 01 · Product + Support

Reminder timing

Three complaints about text reminders arriving at the wrong time.

Risk 02 · CS + Clinic Managers

Three inactive clinics

Clinics 6–8 had zero staff active, putting the adoption goal at risk.

Risk 03 · CS + Sales + Ada

ROI is unclear

The customer needs a defensible story for what the platform is changing.

03 · 90-day plan

Stabilise → re-engage → demonstrate.

The plan starts with the customer blockers, then moves toward adoption and a measurable renewal narrative.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilise

  • Escalate reminder issue
  • Align on ROI
  • Contact inactive clinic managers
  • Share first value summary
DAYS 31–60

Re-engage

  • Train clinics 6–8
  • Confirm reminder fix
  • Close complaints
  • Build per-clinic dashboard
DAYS 61–90

Demonstrate

  • Compile ROI report
  • Pre-brief Ada
  • Deliver EBR
  • Set next 90-day targets

Goals → progress → challenges → next value → actions.

04 · Executive business review

Make the customer the hero.

The EBR is not a presentation of activity. It is a concise story showing what changed, what remains, and what happens next.

The story uses the original business goals: 25% fewer no-shows, 32 front-desk staff activated across eight clinics, and a clear path to renewal value.

05 · Day 90

Build the trajectory — don't fake the finish line.

The Day-90 target is 15% no-show reduction as a credible trajectory toward the 25% goal, not a promise that the full goal has already been achieved.
