How I think

Good customer work connects the dots.

I like work that starts with a real signal and ends with a customer who understands what happens next.

01 · LISTEN FOR THE SIGNAL

Don't just close the ticket.

Look for the pattern behind it; a product friction, adoption gap, process problem or relationship risk.

02 · MAKE IT ACTIONABLE

Bring the right people in.

Carry customer context across Product, Technical and Operations so the customer isn't left coordinating the company.

03 · KEEP VALUE VISIBLE

Show the progress.

Use health, adoption, sentiment and outcomes to make customer value easier to see and act on.
