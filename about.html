About

Customer experience taught me to listen. Customer Success gives me room to act on what I hear.

I have spent my career close to customers and the operational problems behind their complaints. I am now channeling that frontline perspective into Customer Success, with more focus on customer health, adoption, stakeholder alignment, retention and measurable value.

↗ I've supported customers and learners across different time zones, adapting communication and troubleshooting to different customer needs.
The story behind the move

From frontline support to Customer Success.

I'm a customer-focused professional with 4+ years of experience across customer support, customer operations, sales, quality and team leadership.

My experience began on the frontline, where I learned how to listen carefully, solve problems under pressure and understand what customers actually need. As my responsibilities grew, I moved into support leadership, where I managed a seven-person remote team, handled complex escalations, built knowledge systems, analysed recurring customer pain points and worked cross-functionally to improve the customer experience.

That experience naturally led me toward Customer Success, particularly the work that happens beyond solving today's ticket: understanding customer goals, improving adoption, identifying risk, demonstrating value and helping customers achieve outcomes that make them want to stay.

Today, I'm bringing that frontline and operational perspective into Customer Success, combining customer empathy with structured problem-solving, data-informed decision-making and a strong interest in how products can work better for the people using them.

Where I started

Close to the customer.

My foundation is customer support, sales and service operations: listening carefully, solving problems, managing escalations and making sure people are not left wondering what happens next.

What changed

I started seeing the pattern behind the ticket.

As a team lead, I moved beyond individual conversations into Voice of Customer, product feedback, health signals, process improvement, training and cross-functional coordination.

Why Customer Success

I want to stay with the customer beyond the ticket.

Support solves what is happening now. Customer Success gives me the opportunity to understand what the customer is trying to achieve, spot risk earlier and help the relationship move forward.

01 · Understand

Know the goal.

Understand what the customer bought the product to accomplish and what success should look like in their own business.

02 · Guide

Make progress easier.

Coordinate the right internal teams, remove friction and help customers adopt the product in a way that fits their workflow.

03 · Prove

Make value visible.

Use health, adoption, sentiment and business outcomes to show whether the partnership is actually working.

How I work

Calm, structured, human.

I like clear ownership, useful documentation and communication that keeps customers from having to coordinate the company themselves.

Investigate before prescribing.

When the obvious answer isn't the right one.

I separate symptom from cause: product problem, process problem, adoption gap, communication issue or business change. Once that is clear, the plan gets sharper.

Cross-functional by default.

Bring the right people into one customer story.

My experience working with Product, Technical, Authentication, QA and support teams taught me how to carry customer context across functions without losing the thread.
