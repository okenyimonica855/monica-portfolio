About me

Support taught me how to listen. Customer Success is where I want to use it.

I spent the last few years close to customers and closer to the operational problems behind their complaints. Now I'm taking that frontline perspective into Customer Success — with more focus on health, adoption, stakeholder alignment and retention.

What I enjoy solving

When the obvious answer isn't the right one.

I like separating a symptom from a cause: Is this a product problem? A process problem? An adoption gap? A communication issue? Once that is clear, the plan gets much sharper.

How I work

Calm, structured, human.

I care about clear ownership, useful documentation and communication that doesn't make customers do the internal work themselves.

My point of view

Good CS is not just being nice.

It is understanding the customer's business well enough to know what matters, then making progress visible.

01

Investigate.

Don't prescribe training before understanding the blocker.

02

Coordinate.

Bring Product, Support, Technical and Sales into one customer story.

03

Prove value.

Measure behaviour and business outcomes, not activity for activity's sake.
