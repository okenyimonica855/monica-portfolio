Cora × Nubian Skin
Recovering an at-risk customer before renewal.

Cora is the product. Nubian Skin is the customer. This case shows how I would use account health signals to diagnose a decline, coordinate recovery and connect adoption back to customer outcomes.

04 · Investigation

Don't jump from symptom to solution.

Before recommending a solution, I investigated whether the problem was technical, operational, behavioural or business-related.

With Daniel · Technical

Could the workflow be unreliable?

Review tickets, error patterns, affected workflows and configuration changes.

With Amaka · Operations

Where does the team leave Cora?

Follow a real order from channel through fulfilment and identify manual workarounds.

With Stacia · Champion

Is the product still aligned?

Check role changes, expectations, priorities and the original business case.

“The working diagnosis was an unreliable workflow that pushed the team back to spreadsheets and weakened product trust.”

05 · Diagnosis

Fix the blocker before prescribing training.

The working diagnosis was an unreliable workflow that pushed the team back to spreadsheets and weakened product trust.

The recovery therefore starts with reliability and validation — then moves into behaviour change.

06 · 90-day recovery

Three phases. One customer outcome.

The first 30 days focused on fixing the blocker and creating a reliable baseline for recovery.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilise

  • Align stakeholders
  • Escalate and communicate blocker
  • Fix and validate workflow
  • Establish baseline
DAYS 31–60

Rebuild adoption

  • Re-engage users
  • Track WAU and order share
  • Reduce manual work
  • Restore customer cadence
DAYS 61–90

Demonstrate value

  • Review operational outcomes
  • Show efficiency gains
  • Strengthen experience signals
  • Prepare renewal evidence
07 · Value measurement

08 · Renewal readiness

The goal isn't a green health score. It's confidence.

Renewal readiness comes from sustained health, visible value and restored stakeholder confidence.

Technical issue resolved. Adoption recovered. Operational value visible. Relationship restored. Value reviewed. Expansion can be considered only after core health is restored.

“Cora is helping us run the business better.”

That is the outcome the recovery is designed to earn.
